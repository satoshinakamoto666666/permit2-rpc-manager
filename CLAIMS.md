# Epistemic Claims Ledger: Generalized Rank-0 BSD Verification ($N \le 100$)

Every mathematical and computational claim in this repository is tagged with one of four strict epistemic statuses:
- **`PROVED`**: Established by an unconditional mathematical proof together with exact integer or certified interval arithmetic executed by `./verify.sh`.
- **`COMPUTED`**: Certified numerical interval or exact algebraic table verified by `./verify.sh`.
- **`CITED`**: Imported from peer-reviewed theorems or published databases with exact attribution.
- **`OPEN`**: Unproven mathematical conjecture or step outside the scope of this repository (detailed in `OPEN_REMAINDER.md`).
